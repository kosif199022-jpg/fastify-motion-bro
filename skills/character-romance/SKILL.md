## Dual-character cinematic romance
Original stylized family-animation character design, no copyrighted existing character. Create TWO distinct original design locks, one girl/young-adult protagonist and one colorful doll-like character, and a reusable world/lighting bible. Produce a five-scene cohesive love arc. Each scene has AT LEAST TWO independent reference still prompts for different framing/emotion, for ten total images. Narration script is one line per scene, with the same characters/outfits/proportions/world across ALL ten reference prompts. User-supplied photos/reference should take priority; do not invent descriptions of unseen reference people or assets. The CLI returns prompts/story; images and video are not auto-generated. Ask for missing key art preferences when necessary and hold at approval gates before rendering.
